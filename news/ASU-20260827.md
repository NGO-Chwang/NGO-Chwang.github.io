# Second DXH Symposium 2026: From Digital Education to Practice

*August 27, 2026 · Aichi Sangyo University, Okazaki, Japan*

![Second DXH Symposium 2026](images/dxh-symposium-2026.jpg)

*Photo: Your own photograph from the symposium.*

Aichi Sangyo University hosted the Second DXH Symposium 2026, titled **“Towards the Implementation of DX High Schools” (DXハイスクールの実装に向けて)**, on August 27, 2026. The event brought together high school students, university faculty, educators, and industry representatives to explore how digital transformation (DX) education can move from ideas into practical learning.

The symposium focused on sharing educational practices and strengthening **high school–university collaboration (高大連携)**. Student presentations and poster sessions featured projects involving AI, robotics, programming, architectural BIM, and generative AI. The discussions also considered how these activities could develop into university-level project-based learning (PBL) and contribute to the cultivation of future DX talent.

Aichi Prefectural Aichi Comprehensive Technical High School received the top award for its research on robot control using camera-based object detection. Hekinan Technical High School and Nisshin High School received excellence awards for their respective projects.

The event highlighted the importance of connecting student inquiry, technical skills, and real-world challenges through collaboration among schools, universities, businesses, and local communities.
