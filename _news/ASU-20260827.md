On August 27, 2026, Aichi Sangyo University hosted the **Second DXH Symposium 2026**, titled “Towards the Implementation of DX High Schools” (DXハイスクールの実装に向けて). The symposium brought together high school students, university faculty, educators, and industry representatives to discuss the practical implementation of digital transformation (DX) education.

The event featured student research presentations and poster sessions covering AI, robotics, programming, architectural BIM, and generative AI. A central theme was the strengthening of high school–university collaboration (高大連携), connecting student inquiry and technical skills with university-level project-based learning (PBL) and real-world challenges.

During the symposium, Aichi Prefectural Aichi Comprehensive Technical High School received the top award for its research on robot control using camera-based object detection. Hekinan Technical High School and Nisshin High School received excellence awards for their respective projects.

The symposium highlighted the value of collaboration among high schools, universities, businesses, and local communities in developing practical digital skills and cultivating the next generation of DX talent.
